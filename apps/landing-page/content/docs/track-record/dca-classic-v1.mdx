## Overview

**Strategy ID:** `dca_classic`

<StatusBadge capability="dca-benchmark" qualifier="backtest benchmark" />

DCA Classic is a mechanical BTC benchmark. It starts with half of the capital in BTC and half in stablecoins, then moves the stablecoin half into BTC in equal daily amounts across the backtest window. It has no regime awareness, no rebalancing and no discretion.

## Rationale

DCA Classic is a fair baseline: it captures the return of steady BTC accumulation without any active decisions. If the reference strategy cannot beat DCA Classic on a risk-adjusted basis after costs, it has failed its core thesis.

## Parameters

- **Starting allocation:** 50% BTC, 50% stablecoins.
- **Daily step:** the stablecoin half divided by the number of days in the window, moved into BTC once a day until the stablecoins run out.
- **Same backtest assumptions:** the same window, starting capital, fixed slippage and assumed yield as the [reference strategy's backtest](/docs/track-record/dma-fgi-portfolio-rules-v1#backtest-method).

## Comparison

The reference strategy's backtest is compared with DCA Classic on:

1. **Total return** — growth of the starting capital over the window.
2. **Risk-adjusted return** — Sharpe, Sortino and Calmar ratios.
3. **Drawdown** — maximum drawdown over the window.
