# Rednote investment-direction red lines

Four hard rules. Rednote's finance review removes a note that breaks one of them
without reporting anything: the note leaves the manager and its metrics stay at
zero. Each rule has a stable id, and the same ids are what the automated gate
reports back when it asks for a rewrite.

## R1 `asset_allocation_advice`

Never say how much of an asset anyone should hold. No underweight/overweight
verdict, no add/reduce/top-up position, no allocation percentage, no "how much
should you hold" framing — **including when the allocation is attributed to a
named investor**. Quoting a famous name does not make it reporting.

A note that said 「低配債券、超配黃金」 as Ray Dalio's view was removed. The same
fact survives when it is stated without the allocation verb: describe what he
treats gold as, or what risk he is hedging, not the position size he prefers.

## R2 `market_timing_advice`

Never tell a reader when to act. No entry point, exit point, "退場節奏",
"進場時機", take-profit, stop-loss, buy/sell signal, staged entry, or
"what to do when it drops".

A note framed around 「退場節奏」 was removed. Explaining what happened to the
market, or what a participant did and why, is fine; telling the reader when they
should move is not.

## R3 `political_market_speculation`

Politicians, elections, wars and policy are reportable subjects. What is not
allowed is presenting a political actor's motive as the established cause of a
market move — "he needs a win before the election, so he will push crypto up".

If the episode argues it, mark it explicitly as the episode's reading rather than
as fact, and never follow it with a conclusion about what the market will do or
what the reader should do.

## R4 `strong_prediction_unattributed`

A prediction or a strong causal claim — 「被迫買債」、「三年內危機」 — must name
whose claim it is, and must not be stated more strongly than the source states
it. Do not add emotional causation the episode does not contain, and do not
convert someone's scenario into an outcome.

## What is not a risk

The subject matter itself is never a risk. AI companies, agents, hiring and jobs,
Bitcoin, stablecoins, RWA, the Fed, a named politician, a market crash — all are
reportable. These four rules are about giving direction and asserting unattributed
certainty, never about which topic the episode covers. Do not soften or drop a
subject to satisfy them; restate the claim instead.
