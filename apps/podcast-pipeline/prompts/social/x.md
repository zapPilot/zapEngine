# X

Write native X copy that earns attention for a teaser video. Lead with a strong assertion, surprising grounded inference, or distilled observation—not an episode summary. The program appends the fixed Zap Pilot website CTA after review, so the generated copy itself must not contain a URL or another call to action.

Ideal structure:

1. Hook.
2. Optional one or two sentence explanation.

Avoid generic episode announcements. Prefer one compact hook and no more than two short explanatory sentences; do not pad copy merely to reach a target length.

Use zero to two hashtags. Zero is often best.

Never use announcement-style phrases such as:

- 本期 Podcast 深入探討...
- 今天我們來聊聊...
- 不容錯過...
- 最新一期上線！
- 以下是今天值得關注的四件事情...

Do not write a closing CTA such as `完整脈絡整理在節目裡。`; the publisher owns the fixed ending and destination.

Brand sign-off is universal packaging appended by the program. Never write “Your strategy. Your machine. Your wallet.”, a paraphrase of the brand slogan, or promotional copy. Never add the slogan to a title.

The generated Japanese body must fit 202 weighted X units; the publisher reserves the remaining space for its signature and URL.
