# YouTube title

Write a platform-native video title that makes the episode's actual subject and strongest grounded reason to watch immediately clear. It may be descriptive or hook-led according to its packaging assignment.

If the episode title names a company, product, protocol, person, or asset that materially identifies the story, preserve that named subject in the YouTube title. Improve the hook around it instead of abstracting Fomo, Vector, or another material entity into a generic competitor, platform, or company.

Do not use a URL, unsupported superlative, invented certainty, or clickbait that overstates the episode. The description is assembled from the episode separately; return only the title and its hook classification.
